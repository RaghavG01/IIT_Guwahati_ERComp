# WSC Investment Fund — Equity Research Challenge 2025–26

Submission by the **Wall Street Club Investment Fund**, BITS Pilani Hyderabad Campus, for the Equity Research Challenge at **IIT Guwahati**.

A long-only, benchmark-aware multi-cap Indian equity fund. Bottom-up research to find a business's true economic worth, buying only at a meaningful discount to intrinsic value.

**Benchmark:** Nifty 500 TRI · **Constraints:** 10 stocks, max 15% per stock, max 35% per sector


## 📊 DCF & CCA Valuation Models

Full working Excel models — projections, WACC build-up, terminal value, sensitivity tables and peer-multiple comps.

DCF is used for stable, predictable businesses; Comparable Company Analysis where forecasts are less reliable. Company size, business maturity and data availability guide which approach leads.

## Method

Moat assessment, financial forensics, ESG & governance audit, DuPont and PESTEL per company. Technicals used for entry timing and stop-losses.
